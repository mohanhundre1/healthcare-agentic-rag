# Diabetes Monitoring Basics

People living with diabetes often need regular monitoring of blood glucose patterns, A1C, blood pressure, kidney function, cholesterol, foot health, and eye health. The specific schedule should be individualized by the care team.

Education should include nutrition planning, physical activity, medication adherence, recognition of low blood sugar symptoms, and when to seek medical help.

Urgent evaluation is important for severe hypoglycemia, persistent vomiting, confusion, dehydration, chest pain, or trouble breathing.
