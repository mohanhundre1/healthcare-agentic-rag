# Medication Safety Principles

Medication recommendations should account for allergies, pregnancy status, kidney and liver function, age, other medicines, recent lab values, and the patient's treatment goals.

Patients should not start, stop, or change prescription medication doses based only on automated advice. Dose changes should be confirmed by a licensed clinician who can review the full medical history.

Emergency help may be needed for suspected overdose, severe allergic reaction, trouble breathing, swelling of the face or throat, or loss of consciousness.
