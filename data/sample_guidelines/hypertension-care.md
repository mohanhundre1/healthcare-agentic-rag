# Hypertension Care Basics

Adults with elevated blood pressure should be encouraged to monitor blood pressure regularly, review home readings with a clinician, and discuss cardiovascular risk factors such as smoking, diabetes, cholesterol, kidney disease, diet, and physical activity.

Lifestyle support commonly includes reducing sodium intake, increasing physical activity when appropriate, limiting alcohol, managing weight, and following a heart-healthy eating pattern.

Medication decisions depend on the patient's overall risk, readings over time, comorbid conditions, adverse effects, and clinician assessment. Patients should not stop blood pressure medication without discussing it with a clinician.
